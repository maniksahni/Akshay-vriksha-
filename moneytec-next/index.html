Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to start a SIP?

Many schemes take SIPs from ₹500 a month. The amount can grow with your income; starting and staying consistent matter most.

Where does my money actually go?

Straight from your bank account to the fund house (AMC) or the insurer. Units and policies are in your name, and it never passes through Moneytec.

Can I stop or pause my SIP?

Yes. Most SIPs can be paused, changed or stopped any time. Tax-saving (ELSS) funds lock each instalment for 3 years, and Gulshan flags that upfront.

What documents do I need to invest in mutual funds?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar (or passport for NRIs), a bank account and a photo. KYC is done once and works across fund houses, mostly online.

I live abroad. Can I invest in Indian mutual funds?

Yes, through NRE or NRO accounts. Residents of the US and Canada can invest with select fund houses only, and Gulshan will tell you which.

How does Moneytec get paid?

As an AMFI-registered distributor (ARN-105840) and Kotak Life advisor, Moneytec earns commission from fund houses and the insurer, as regulated by SEBI, AMFI and IRDAI. There is no separate advisory fee, and this is shared upfront.
