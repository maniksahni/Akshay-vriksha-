Mutual fund KYC, explained

KYC (Know Your Customer) is an identity and address check that you complete before investing in mutual funds in India. It is done once and works across fund houses, mostly online.

Documents you usually need

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar, or a passport for NRIs
  • A bank account in your name
  • A photo

How the process works

  1. Fill in your details through a fund house, a KRA or your distributor.
  2. Verify your identity, commonly through Aadhaar-based verification and an in-person or video check.
  3. Sign digitally and submit.
  4. The KYC Registration Agency (KRA) records your status, and fund houses can then rely on it.

What your KYC status means

StatusWhat it generally means
ValidatedYour records have been verified and you can generally invest across fund houses
RegisteredYou can generally continue, but a fresh check may be needed for a new fund house
On hold or rejectedSomething needs to be corrected before you can invest

You can check your status on a KRA website using your PAN. Status names can change, so ask if you are not sure what yours means.

When you may need to update it

  • Your name, address, mobile number or email has changed.
  • Your PAN and Aadhaar need linking.
  • You have become an NRI or have returned to India.

KYC for NRIs

NRIs usually need a passport and overseas address proof along with PAN, plus FATCA details. See the NRI investment guide.

Never share your OTP or banking passwords with anyone for KYC, including a distributor. Moneytec never collects your investment money.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need KYC for each mutual fund?

No. KYC is done once and works across fund houses, although some status types may need an update.

How long does mutual fund KYC take?

Online KYC is often quick to submit, and verification can take a few working days.

How do I check my KYC status?

Enter your PAN on a KYC Registration Agency (KRA) website, or ask your distributor to check it with you.
