How Moneytec works

Moneytec is the practice of Gulshan Kohli, an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor (ARN-105840) and Kotak Life advisor (KLI61205587) in Rohini, New Delhi. It starts with a conversation and moves only when you are ready.

What we do

  • Help you start a SIP and complete KYC
  • Help you plan a monthly amount for a goal
  • Go through your existing investments in a portfolio review
  • Discuss NRI investing and what is available for your country
  • Explore life cover through Kotak Life

How it goes, step by step

  1. Talk: message or call Gulshan. You speak with him personally.
  2. Understand: your goal, timeline, income, commitments and comfort with risk.
  3. Review the details: costs, risks and scheme or policy documents.
  4. You decide: nothing moves without your approval.
  5. Set up: KYC, then your payment goes directly to the fund house or insurer.
  6. Keep in touch: review as your life and goals change.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your investment or premium. Your money never touches Moneytec.
  • We do not promise returns. Illustrations on this site are not forecasts.
  • We do not ask for your OTP or banking passwords.
  • Nothing is invested or booked from this website; the forms only prepare a WhatsApp message for you to send.

Where your money goes

Straight from your bank account to the fund house (AMC) or the insurer. Mutual fund units and policies are in your name, subject to the product terms.

How we are paid

As an AMFI-registered distributor and Kotak Life advisor, Moneytec earns commission from fund houses and the insurer, as regulated by SEBI, AMFI and IRDAI. There is no separate advisory fee, and this is shared upfront.

Frequently asked questions

Does Moneytec charge a fee?

There is no separate advisory fee. Moneytec earns commission from fund houses and the insurer, as regulated by SEBI, AMFI and IRDAI, and shares this upfront.

Does my money go to Moneytec?

No. It goes directly from your bank account to the fund house or insurer, and the units or policies are in your name.

Can I invest through the website?

No. Nothing is invested here. The website helps you explore, and its forms open a WhatsApp message for you to send.
