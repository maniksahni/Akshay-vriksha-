Check a distributor before you start

Anyone selling mutual funds in India must hold an ARN (AMFI Registration Number). You can look up ARN-105840 yourself on the AMFI distributor locator. Before you work with any distributor, it is worth asking:

  • What is your ARN, and is it currently valid?
  • How are you paid on what you are suggesting?
  • Does my money go directly to the fund house?
  • Will the units be in my own name?

Your money never passes through Moneytec

Your investment goes from your bank account straight to the fund house (AMC) or the insurer. Mutual fund units and policies are in your name, subject to the product terms. This website does not collect investments or premiums.
