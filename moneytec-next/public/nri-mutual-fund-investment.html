NRI mutual fund investment in India

Yes, NRIs can invest in Indian mutual funds, including through SIPs. Investments are made through an NRE or NRO account in rupees. Residents of the US and Canada can invest with select fund houses only.

01

NRE or NRO account?

NRE accountNRO account
Typically used forMoney earned outside IndiaIncome earned in India, such as rent or pension
Taking money back abroadGenerally freely repatriableRepatriable within limits, subject to rules and paperwork

Which one suits you depends on where the money came from and whether you may want to move it abroad later. Check the current rules with your bank before you invest.

02

Documents you usually need

  • PAN
  • Passport, and overseas address proof
  • NRE or NRO bank account details
  • A photo
  • FATCA details about your tax residency

KYC requirements can differ between fund houses, and some steps can be completed online.

03

If you live in the US or Canada

Only select fund houses accept investments from residents of the US and Canada, and they may ask for extra declarations. Check which ones accept you before you choose a scheme. Gulshan will tell you which fund houses are available for you.

04

Tax

Gains can be taxed in India, tax may be deducted at source, and your country of residence may also tax them. A double-taxation agreement may help. Ask a tax professional who knows both countries, as rules change and depend on your situation.

05

Investing from abroad

A monthly SIP can run from your NRE or NRO account. You do not need to be in India for each instalment. You can use the SIP calculator to compare amounts in rupees; currency movements can change what those rupees are worth to you abroad.

06

Frequently asked questions

Can NRIs invest in Indian mutual funds?

Yes, through NRE or NRO accounts. Residents of the US and Canada can invest with select fund houses only.

Can NRIs start a SIP in India?

Yes. A SIP can usually run from an NRE or NRO bank account after KYC is complete.

Can I invest from the USA or Canada?

Yes, but only with select fund houses that accept residents of those countries, and extra declarations may be needed.

What documents does an NRI need?

Usually PAN, a passport, overseas address proof, NRE or NRO account details, a photo and FATCA details. Requirements can vary between fund houses.
