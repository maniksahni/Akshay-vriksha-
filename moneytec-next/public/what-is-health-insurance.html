What is health insurance?

Health insurance helps pay medical costs when you are treated for a covered illness or injury, usually in hospital, up to the amount you are insured for. You pay a premium, typically every year, and the insurer pays eligible costs under the policy terms.

01

Why it is different from life insurance

Life insurance protects your family if you die. Health insurance helps pay for treatment while you are alive. Many families look at both when planning for the unexpected.

02

Two common ways to set up cover

Individual policyFamily floater
CoverEach person has their own sum insuredThe family shares one sum insured
Think aboutOlder members or specific health needsOne large claim can use up cover for everyone that year

03

How claims work

  • Cashless: at a network hospital, the insurer settles approved costs directly with the hospital.
  • Reimbursement: you pay first, then claim the eligible amount from the insurer with your bills and documents.

04

Terms worth understanding

TermMeaning
Sum insuredThe most the policy pays in a policy year, subject to its terms
Waiting periodTime before some conditions are covered, such as pre-existing illnesses
Room rent limitA cap on room cost; a higher room can reduce other payouts too
Co-payThe share of an approved claim you pay yourself
DeductibleThe amount you pay before the insurer pays
Network hospitalA hospital that has an arrangement with the insurer for cashless claims
ExclusionsWhat the policy does not cover

05

Before you buy

  • Disclose your full medical history honestly.
  • Check waiting periods, limits and sub-limits, not only the premium.
  • Check whether nearby hospitals are in the network.
  • Renew on time so that you do not lose continuity benefits.

07

Frequently asked questions

What is cashless health insurance?

At a network hospital, the insurer settles approved treatment costs directly with the hospital, so you pay only amounts that are not covered.

What is a waiting period?

A set time after buying a policy before certain conditions, such as pre-existing illnesses, are covered. It differs by policy.

Is employer health cover enough?

It can help, but it usually ends when you leave the job and the amount may be limited. Check it against your family’s needs.
