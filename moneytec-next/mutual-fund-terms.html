Mutual fund terms explained

Every term you will see on Moneytec and in most scheme documents, explained in plain English.

01

The basics

TermMeaning
AMCAsset Management Company: the fund house that runs a mutual fund.
SchemeA specific mutual fund, with its own objective and portfolio.
UnitYour share in a scheme.
NAVNet Asset Value: the price of one unit, usually declared each business day.
RedemptionSelling your units back to get your money.
FolioYour account number with a fund house.
NomineeThe person you name to receive your investments if you die.

02

Ways of investing

TermMeaning
SIPSystematic Investment Plan: a fixed amount invested regularly.
Lump sumInvesting a single larger amount at once.
STPSystematic Transfer Plan: moving money regularly from one scheme to another.
SWPSystematic Withdrawal Plan: withdrawing a set amount regularly.
Step-up SIPA SIP whose amount rises at set intervals.
Growth optionGains stay invested and are reflected in the NAV.
IDCW optionIncome Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal: payouts that may be made from the scheme, and which reduce the NAV.

03

Returns and costs

TermMeaning
CAGRCompound Annual Growth Rate: a smoothed yearly growth rate between two points. Real returns are uneven.
XIRRA yearly return calculation for several cash flows at different dates, such as a SIP.
Expense ratioThe scheme’s yearly cost as a percentage of its assets, already reflected in the NAV.
Exit loadA charge some schemes apply if you redeem within a set period.
RiskometerA scale from Low to Very High that shows a scheme’s risk level.
BenchmarkAn index that a scheme’s performance is compared with.
InflationThe general rise in prices, which reduces what money can buy.

04

Fund types and plans

TermMeaning
Equity fundInvests mainly in company shares.
Debt fundInvests mainly in bonds and money-market instruments.
Hybrid fundInvests in a mix of equity and debt.
ELSSEquity Linked Savings Scheme: an equity fund with a 3-year lock-in.
Index fundAims to follow a market index.
Direct planYou invest without a distributor; lower expense ratio.
Regular planYou invest through a distributor, who earns commission; higher expense ratio.

People, rules and accounts

TermMeaning

ARNAMFI Registration Number: a distributor’s registration. Gulshan Kohli’s is ARN-105840.
IRDAIInsurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India: the insurance regulator.
KYCKnow Your Customer: the identity check you complete before investing.
PANPermanent Account Number: your income-tax ID, needed to invest.
NRINon-Resident Indian.
NRE accountA rupee account generally used for money earned outside India.
NRO accountA rupee account generally used for income earned in India.
FATCAA tax-residency declaration required when you invest.

06

Frequently asked questions

What is NAV in a mutual fund?

Net Asset Value is the price of one unit of a scheme. It is calculated from the value of the scheme’s assets, less liabilities, divided by the number of units.

What is the difference between direct and regular plans?

In a direct plan you invest without a distributor and the expense ratio is lower. In a regular plan a distributor earns commission and the expense ratio is higher.

What is CAGR?

Compound Annual Growth Rate is the smoothed yearly rate that takes a value from its start to its end. Actual yearly returns can be very different.
