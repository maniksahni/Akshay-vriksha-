What is life insurance?

Life insurance is a contract: you pay a premium, and if the insured person dies during the policy term, the insurer pays the agreed amount to the nominee, subject to the policy terms. It is meant to protect the people who depend on your income.

The main types

TypeWhat it doesGood to know
Term insurancePays a death benefit during the termUsually gives high cover for the premium; standard plans pay nothing if you outlive the term
Endowment and savings plansCombine cover with a maturity benefitHigher premium; compare the benefits carefully
ULIPsCombine cover with market-linked investmentHave charges, market risk and a lock-in
Whole-life plansProvide cover for most of your lifeTerms vary by plan

How much cover might you need?

A common way to think about it, before you get a personal assessment, is to add up what your family would need:

  • Household expenses for the years they would depend on your income
  • Outstanding loans, such as a home loan
  • Future goals, such as children’s education

Then subtract existing cover and savings that could be used. The right cover also depends on affordability, health and eligibility.

Before you buy

  • Tell the insurer everything: health, smoking, occupation and existing policies. Non-disclosure can affect a claim.
  • Read the exclusions: know what the policy does not cover.
  • Name your nominee and tell your family that the policy exists.
  • Check the premium period and what happens if you miss a payment.
  • Use the free-look period: you can review the policy after you receive it and return it within the stated period, subject to its terms.

How a claim generally works

  1. The nominee informs the insurer.
  2. They submit the claim form, death certificate, policy and identity documents.
  3. The insurer reviews the claim under the policy terms and decides.

Frequently asked questions

What is term insurance?

Life cover for a fixed period. If the insured person dies during the term, the insurer pays the nominee, subject to the policy terms. Standard term plans pay nothing if the person outlives the term.

Is life insurance an investment?

Its main purpose is protection. Some plans include savings or market-linked features, so compare their charges, benefits and lock-ins carefully.

Who should consider life insurance?

Generally anyone whose income supports others, such as a spouse, children or parents, or who has loans the family would need to repay.
